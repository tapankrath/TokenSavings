        How these estimates work

        I start from what you pay. Each change is estimated as a share of your bill using general assumptions, such as resending less context trimming part of what you pay to send, or shorter answers trimming part of what you pay to receive. I show a range because real results depend on your provider, your plan and your habits.

        I can’t see your real usage or invoices, so treat this as a starting point. Try one change at a time and check your next bill.

        Directional estimates only. Savings are not guaranteed and depend on your provider, plan and how you use AI. Prices and features change, so check your provider's current pricing. This is general information, not financial advice.
